## Q1. Which solo / tiny-team mobile games have credible evidence of US$10k+/month?

### Takeaway
Only a handful of solo or tiny-team games have **public** evidence of US$10k+/month, and almost none of it is audited:
- **Magic Research** (solo, idle; self-reported, ~US$400k in 12 months)
- **BitLife** (two founders, text life-sim; acquirer-disclosed US$26M in 2019)
- **Egg, Inc.** (idle; third-party estimate >US$1M/month)
- **Balatro** (solo designer, but a publisher-ported premium hit)
- **fly.pieter.com** (solo, vibe-coded, but a web game rather than mobile)

The biggest "simple puzzle" hits (Block Blast and similar) come from studios with hundreds of staff, so they show how big a genre can get, not what a solo dev can expect. For most simple genres I found **no credible solo-dev revenue source**. The 2025 base rate for newcomers is harsh: only **1.4%** of new casual titles passed US$100k/month in H1 2025.

### Inferences
- **Credible solo / tiny-team US$10k+/month evidence clusters in three shapes:**
  1. **UI/text-driven idle and sim games with deep progression** (Magic Research, BitLife, Egg, Inc.). These are mostly IAP or premium.
  2. **Premium or deep design hits** (Balatro). These are not one-month builds.
  3. **Web games with novelty/virality** (fly.pieter.com, Clues by Sam, Bracket City).

  The genres a solo dev most often thinks of (block blast, screw sort, water sort, tile match) show **studio-scale** revenue (Block Blast ~US$17.5M/month est.), and I found **no public solo case** in them.
- **The most "AI-buildable" category with proven tiny-team economics is the UI/text life-sim or idle genre (BitLife, Magic Research).** It needs no physics engine, the content can be drafted with LLM help, and it monetizes through IAP rather than needing huge DAU for ads.
- **Daily-puzzle web games build audience but do not show public revenue.** Their value shows up as **acquisitions by publishers** (Bracket City → The Atlantic). For a solo dev, a daily puzzle is a **retention and brand engine**, not proven direct revenue.
- **Party word games (imposter, charades)** rank highly (93K ratings, #2 Family) and take days to build. But they are now **heavily cloned** (see Q4) and have no public revenue.

## Q2. Monetization: ads vs IAP vs subscription vs "remove ads", and realistic revenue per DAU

### Takeaway
On a low budget with little traffic:
- **Ads alone rarely reach US$10k/month.** Ads-only casual games make roughly **US$0.01–0.05 per DAU per day**, so US$10k/month needs about **6,700–33,000 DAU**.
- **Hybrid monetization lowers the DAU needed to roughly 700–2,200.** That means rewarded ads plus a "remove ads" IAP, plus a subscription or IAP where the genre allows, at about **US$0.15–0.50 ARPDAU**.
- The documented solo successes lean on **IAP or premium** (Magic Research, Egg, Inc., BitLife, Balatro) or on **direct sponsorship** (fly.pieter.com).
- Rewarded video is the best-paying ad format in Tier-1 markets. Australia is among the highest-paying ad markets.

### Cited Findings
- **Ad prices (eCPM, Appodeal, Q4 2024 data; report labelled 2025):**
  - Rewarded video, US: **US$19.63 on iOS**, **US$16.49 on Android**.
  - Rewarded video, Australia: **#1 on Android at US$18.87**.
  - Interstitial, US: **US$14.32 on iOS**, **US$14.08 on Android**.
  - A different write-up gives **Australia interstitial US$9.55 iOS / US$8.67 Android**. The sources conflict.
  - Q4 is seasonally the highest quarter.
  - Sources: [Appodeal mobile eCPM report Q4 2024](https://appodeal.com/the-mobile-ecpm-report-updated-q4-2024-view); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/marketing/item-16173); [Business of Apps](https://businessofapps.com/?p=85258)

### Inferences
- **DAU needed for US$10k/month (≈US$333/day):**

  | Model | ARPDAU | DAU needed |
  |---|---|---|
  | Ads-only casual (low end) | US$0.01 | ~33,000 |
  | Ads-only casual (high end) | US$0.05 | ~6,700 |
  | Hybrid casual (low end) | US$0.15 | ~2,200 |
  | Hybrid casual (high end) | US$0.50 | ~670 |

  These are arithmetic on the cited ranges, not forecasts.
- **Illustrative per-player ad maths (Tier-1 player):**
  - Inputs: 2 rewarded views at ~US$15 eCPM plus 3 interstitials at ~US$10 eCPM per day.
  - Result: ≈ **US$0.06 per DAU per day**.
  - A global player mix (lots of low-eCPM countries) pulls this down to the cited US$0.01–0.05.
  - Implication: a solo dev with modest organic traffic should **skew toward Tier-1 English markets** (US, UK, CA, AU) and lean on **rewarded** formats.
- **Suggested stack for the stated constraints** (solo, ≤US$1k, evergreen, low traffic):
  1. Free download.
  2. **Rewarded ads** for hints, undo, extra daily puzzles, streak repair.
  3. A light, capped interstitial between sessions (not after every level).
  4. A one-off **"Remove ads" IAP**.
  5. An optional **subscription or premium tier** for archives and unlimited puzzles, the NYT-Games-style daily model.

  Reasoning: ad-only needs DAU the dev can't buy on this budget, while IAP/subscription monetizes a small loyal base. Idle or text-sim designs suit IAP best (the Egg, Inc., BitLife and Magic Research pattern).
- **Retention compounds the DAU problem:** with median 2025 retention (D30 <1%, see Q3), sustaining ~7k DAU needs a continuous inflow of thousands of installs a day. Organic virality is therefore the real bottleneck, not ad rates.

- No **"remove ads" IAP conversion-rate** benchmark found.

## Q3. What drives addictiveness and retention?

### Takeaway
The 2025 retention baseline is brutal:
- **Median game: D1 ~22%, D7 <4%, D30 ~0.7–0.8%.**
- **Top 10%: ~40% / ~12% / ~4%.**
- Retention declined again in 2025.

Documented solo successes retain through:
- **Daily ritual plus shareable results** (Clues by Sam reached 50k+ daily players within months; Bracket City was acquired within a year).
- **Long progression loops** (idle games: Egg, Inc., Idle Slayer, Magic Research).
- **Deep replayable runs** (Balatro).

Juice and polish matter, but I found little sourced quantitative evidence on individual mechanics.

### Cited Findings
- **GameAnalytics 2025 retention (16,000+ mobile games):**
  - Median: **~22% D1, <4% D7, 0.7–0.8% D30**.
  - Top 10%: **~40% D1, ~12% D7, ~4% D30**.
  - **Retention declined again in 2025.**
  - Sources: [GameGrowthAdvisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-mobile-game-kpis-benchmarks-2026/); [InvestGame – mobile retention benchmarks 2026 (PDF)](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-01-20-Mobile_retention_benchmarks_2026.pdf)

- **Daily-puzzle format retains audiences at solo scale:**
  - Clues by Sam (single designer, launched May 2025) reported **>50,000 daily active players by early 2026**. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clues_By_Sam); [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/clues-by-sam-wordle-daily-puzzle-game/)
  - Bracket City (launched 1 Jan 2025) was picked up by **The Atlantic** in 2025. — [perrotta.dev](https://perrotta.dev/2025/04/bracket-city/)
- **Community-driven idle retention:** Magic Research grew from **two Reddit posts in r/incremental_games**, a niche community of engaged idle players. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/anulagarwal12/a-solo-game-developer-generated-over-400000-in-12-months-from-his-game-with-only-2-reddit-posts-5ahn)
- **Idle progression scale:** Egg, Inc. holds an est. **4M MAU** years after launch. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/993492744)

### Inferences
- **Retention levers that are cheap to build and fit a solo AI-assisted build** (design knowledge, not measured here):
  1. **One daily puzzle shared by everyone**, plus a **spoiler-free emoji/grid share card**. This is the Wordle mechanic, and it doubles as free acquisition.
  2. **Streaks with a forgiving "streak freeze"**, which can be monetized via rewarded ads.
  3. **Archive or "unlimited" mode** behind a subscription or IAP.
  4. **Progressive difficulty across the week** (easy Monday, hard Sunday).
  5. **Local push reminders.**
  6. **Juice:** haptics, sound, particle bursts, satisfying clear animations. React Native can do this with Reanimated, Skia and Expo Haptics.
  7. **Light competition:** friends or global daily leaderboards via Game Center / Play Games, or a simple backend.
- **Idle/incremental adds:**
  - Offline earnings.
  - Prestige resets (exponential numbers).
  - Very long progression, which suits IAP.
- **Implication from the median curve:** without a share loop or daily ritual, an organically launched simple puzzle will lose more than 99% of installers within a month. Building the **share card and daily mode first** probably matters more than adding levels.

## Q6. Buildability: can AI app builders ship a smooth puzzle/word game to both stores in ~1 month? Which types need an engine? Any documented vibe-coded money-makers?

### Takeaway
**Yes for grid, tile, word, logic, card-light, idle/text and party games.** These are effectively animated UI, a good fit for React Native/Expo (and so for Rork Pro, Bolt/Expo and similar builders), ideally with **Reanimated plus Skia** for smooth 60fps animation. A solo dev using an AI builder plus an AI code editor can realistically ship one in a month.

**No (or risky) for physics-heavy, real-time action, 3D, or "satisfying" particle/ASMR games** (screw sort 3D, ball physics, survivors-likes). Those warrant **Godot (free) or Unity**. AI can still write much of the code, but not through a no-code app builder.

Documented vibe-coded game money-makers are almost all **web games** (fly.pieter.com). I found **no verified vibe-coded App Store / Google Play game** earning meaningful revenue. Dedicated mobile vibe-coding apps themselves "failed to gain traction" by Sept 2025.
