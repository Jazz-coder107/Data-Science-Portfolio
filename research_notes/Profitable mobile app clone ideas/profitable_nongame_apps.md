# Profitable non-game consumer mobile apps from solo founders and tiny teams (2024–2026): examples, creators, teardowns, saturation, build cost

**How this was researched (read first):** In this session WebFetch failed with DNS errors for every domain tried (starterstory.com, techcrunch.com, indiehackers.com, apps.apple.com), and direct curl was blocked by the egress proxy. So **no page was read in full**. Every finding below comes from search-engine result summaries of the linked pages. Treat wording as paraphrase, not verbatim quotes. The web-search budget also ran out before a few planned checks (QUITTR pricing, Coconote details, document-scanner and AI-journal markets). Those are listed under Gaps. Today's date is Oct 12, 2026, and anything dated before 2025 is labelled OLDER.

Labels used: **VERIFIED** means revenue was pulled from a payment-provider API by a third-party site (TrustMRR/whatsthe.app/ProvenMRR via RevenueCat). **ACQUIRER-REPORTED** means the figure came from the buyer, so it is not audited. **SELF-REPORTED** means the founder said it in an interview, a post or a video, including dashboards shown on camera. **ESTIMATE** means a modelled figure from Sensor Tower, Appfigures, AppGoblin, Apprank, ScreensDesign or Starter Story's own estimates.

## 1. Which specific apps and founders report ~US$10k+/month, and how trustworthy is each number?

### Takeaway
I found 18 real non-game consumer apps or app portfolios with about $10k+/month reported, plus several below-threshold counterexamples. Only a handful are truly **VERIFIED**. The best of those for the user's purposes is **TrackAI**, a solo-led AI photo calorie counter at about $20.5k MRR verified through RevenueCat on TrustMRR in mid-2026. Most famous examples (Cal AI, QUITTR, Umax, Prayer Lock, Wrestle AI, Payout, ONCE) are **SELF-REPORTED**, and several of their numbers conflict across sources. The examples that match the user's constraints most closely (non-coder, AI/no-code builder, about $1k budget) are **Wrestle AI**, built with Rork for about $1,100 total spend, and **Prayer Lock**, which the founder says he built in 3 days with AI tools.

### Inferences
- **Health and fitness AI scanning** (calorie photo apps) is the only category with both a mega-outcome (Cal AI) **and** an independently verified solo-scale outcome (TrackAI, ~$20.5K MRR). The TrustMRR long tail (FitCal $5.6K, then several apps under $100 MRR) shows most clones fail. A clone in this category has a real but narrow chance.
- The founders with the most "clone-and-improve" DNA are Mau Baron (he copied Praycreen and improved it) and Brian Shin (he took the disposable-camera concept to events). Both reached about $20K/month within roughly 3–6 months, according to their own reports.
- Nearly every big number here is self-reported or comes from a vendor or seller with a reason to inflate it (Rork, Superwall, Starter Story, Acquire listings). Only the TrustMRR/RevenueCat numbers are independent of the founder's narrative.
- Many winners are **not "AI wrappers"** at heart. HabitKit, Puff Count, ONCE and Payout are simple utilities. In those apps AI helped with the build or the marketing more than it powers the product.

### Gaps
- QUITTR's current (2026) revenue is unknown, and the sources conflict by about 10x ($50K vs $500K/month).
- Umax's 2025–2026 revenue was not found. The latest dated figure is from mid-2024.
- The Prayer Lock interview date and the year of the Oct–Dec numbers are not confirmed. They are most likely Oct–Dec 2025.
- I could not see which founders or team sizes sit behind the TrustMRR mobile-category leaders (Composer.AI, Project A, a "Stealth Venture" health & fitness iOS app at ~$95K MRR, and "Taller" at ~$39K MRR) — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/category/mobile-apps).
- I found no indie, solo-scale revenue example with a source for these categories: AI document scanners, AI journals/companions, posture apps, AI caption/thumbnail mobile apps, or mushroom/insect identifiers.

## 3. Teardowns of the most clone-friendly apps (core feature, onboarding, paywall, pricing, acquisition, 1–3★ complaints, competitors)

### Takeaway
The seven most clone-friendly concepts are: (A) AI photo calorie scanner, (B) quit-habit tracker, (C) prayer-gated app blocker, (D) AI face-rating/glow-up, (E) niche-sport AI video coach, (F) "money finder" utility and (G) collectible identifier. Across them, the recurring improvement openings in low-star reviews are the same: **billing and cancellation pain, paywalls before any value, inaccurate or inconsistent AI output, scan/processing bugs and unresponsive support.** A clone that is honest about price, shows value before the paywall, fixes reliability and answers support could beat incumbents on reviews.

### Cited Findings

**A. AI photo calorie scanner (Cal AI; TrackAI as the solo comparator)**
- *Core feature:* photograph a meal and get estimated calories and macros. This is vendor-reported — [Superwall case study](https://superwall.com/case-studies/cal-ai).
- *Paywall and pricing:* a hard paywall with a usually 3-day trial. Reported prices: ~$2.99/week; monthly $5.99–$19.99 (most often $9.99); annual $19.99–$49.99 (e.g., $29.99). Prices vary through A/B and regional tests — [AI Insights News](https://aiinsightsnews.net/is-cal-ai-free/); [NutriScan](https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/cal-ai-pricing-2026-monthly-yearly-premium-abc6e7b26f). ### Inferences
- **Clone-and-improve priorities by concept:**
  - (A) Calorie: offer a transparent price with a real trial, make photo results editable by ingredient with portion sliders, and give a single-tap cancel/support path.
  - (B) Quit: show the value first (a few days free), reliable restore-purchase for lifetime plans, local-only or private journaling, and voice/shortcut logging.
  - (C) Prayer lock: a usable free tier, rock-solid scheduling, meaningful content after paying (guided prayers, plans), and responsive support.
  - (D) Face rating: deterministic or consistent scoring (same photo, same score), no bait-and-switch paywall, and kinder positioning.
- Hard paywalls with weekly plans are the norm in these niches. Apple's April 2026 action against Cal AI suggests that the "weekly-equivalent price emphasis" and "decline → second offer" patterns now carry **App Review risk** for a newcomer copying them.
- Concept E (niche-sport coach) is the most "underserved sub-niche" template. One sport plus one influencer partner gave a no-code founder ~$17–23K/month (self-reported).

### Gaps
- QUITTR's exact prices (weekly/annual/lifetime) were not retrieved before the search budget ran out.
- Onboarding quiz lengths are only documented for QUITTR (40 steps). Cal AI is documented only as "quiz before price". Prayer Lock, Wrestle AI and Payout onboarding are undocumented.
- I found no low-star review analysis for TrackAI, Wrestle AI, Payout, ONCE or the identifier apps.
- A claimed "25–50% variance on mixed dishes" for Cal AI appeared in search summaries of competitor blogs, but I could not attribute it to a specific page, so I excluded it.

## 4. Saturation assessment per category

### Takeaway
The most saturated categories are AI calorie scanners, plant identifiers, homework scanners, AI caption/video tools, face rating and generic habit trackers. These have dozens of clones, studio or VC incumbents, or acquirer-backed leaders. The faith app-blocker niche went from one app to 8+ clones within about a year. ## 5. Build difficulty with an AI/no-code builder in ~4 weeks, API dependencies and per-user cost (estimates flagged)

### Takeaway
Most of these concepts are buildable by one person with an AI/no-code mobile builder in about 4 weeks. The proof point is Wrestle AI: Rork + Supabase + OpenAI, about $1,100 total spend, under $150/month to run, and $250 to a Fiverr developer for payments and auth. Vision-API cost per photo scan is a fraction of a cent (estimate), so the real costs are marketing and the paywall/subscription plumbing. The exceptions are app blockers that need iOS Screen Time APIs (harder for no-code) and data-curation utilities like Payout (ongoing work).

### Cited Findings
- Wrestle AI was built without code using Rork, Supabase and OpenAI. A Fiverr developer was paid about $250 for payments and authentication, monthly costs stay under $150 and total spend was about $1,100 — [Rork case study](https://rork.com/blog/george-wrestle-ai-case-study) (vendor-reported).
- Prayer Lock was reportedly built "in just three days using AI tools" by a founder with no tech background — [Startup Finder](https://www.startupfinder.co/posts/how-prayer-lock-grew-to-20kmonth-in-six-months-ybjc).
- Puff Count's founder had no coding experience and used an outsourced team — [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/24-year-old-earns-40k-monthly-building-apps-no-coding-experience-heres-how-he-does-it-1728502); [OEB Digital](https://oebdigital.com/cases/puff-count-turning-a-mission-driven-app-into-a-clean-confident-exit/).
- A 2026 roundup says a typical vibe-coded indie mobile app costs about $50–300/month to run, and that tools such as Vibecode and Replit Mobile let founders build and submit apps from a phone — [MakeAnAppLike](https://makeanapplike.com/article/list/top-10-vibe-coded-apps).
- RevenueCat is free until $2,500 in monthly tracked revenue. Third-party comparisons say it then charges 1% of gross — [RevenueCat Indies](https://www.revenuecat.com/indies); [BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/revenuecat-vs-superwall-vs-adapty-in-app-subscription-indie-2026).
- GPT-4o mini's official price is $0.15 per 1M input tokens and $0.60 per 1M output tokens, and it accepts image input — [OpenAI docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-4o-mini). A secondary guide says a 1024×1024 image is about 765 tokens — [aifreeapi](https://www.aifreeapi.com/en/posts/gpt-4o-pricing-per-million-tokens). Gemini Flash pricing is listed under Gaps because I could not tie it to a specific source page.
- Another plant-ID build story used Replit plus the Gemini API and Stripe. It is an unsourced Medium post, and its "$2M" headline is unverified — [Medium](https://medium.com/@moneytent/how-i-built-a-2m-plant-identification-app-using-ai-in-simple-steps-633ae149b14d).

### Inferences
- With about $1,000 total, the binding constraints are **Apple/Google developer fees, a builder subscription, possibly a $250-ish freelancer for paywall/auth integration (the Wrestle AI precedent), and organic or UGC marketing**, not AI inference.
- The cheapest-to-serve and fastest-to-build concepts are A (calorie scanner), B (quit tracker), D (face rating) and G (identifier). Of these, B and a localized or niche variant of A or C look like the best balance of evergreen demand and newcomer competitiveness. C needs native screen-time APIs, so confirm that the chosen builder supports them before committing.
- AI apps "churn 30% faster" (RevenueCat 2026), so an AI wrapper needs a retention loop (streaks, plans, progress) beyond one-shot scans. This favours designs that mix tracking with AI rather than pure "scan and done".
